# Privacy Policy for DoIt

**Effective Date: August 20, 2025**

At **DoIt**, we believe that your productivity should not come at the cost of your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information within the application.

---

## 1. Data Collection and Storage

DoIt is a **"Local-First"** application. This means:

* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or share your name to use the app.
* **Local Storage:** All your tasks, descriptions, history, and progress levels are stored locally on your device using an internal database (SQLite).
* **No Cloud Sync:** We do not upload your tasks or personal schedules to any external servers or cloud services. Your data stays on your phone.

---

## 2. App Permissions

To provide a premium experience, the app requests the following permissions:

* **Notifications:** Used solely to send you reminders for your tasks. You can enable or disable this at any time in the Settings.
* **Storage/File Access:** Used to save your settings preferences and manage local database operations.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

* **GitHub Fetching:** The app connects to GitHub to fetch the latest version of this Privacy Policy and to check for application updates. No personal identifiers or device-specific data are sent during this request.
* **Flutter Downloader:** Used only if you choose to download updates. This process is governed by the standard security protocols of your device's operating system.

---

## 4. Security

We prioritize the security of your local data. However, since the data is stored on your device, the security of your information also depends on your device's security settings, such as passcodes, PINs, or biometrics.

---

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app. Since we do not collect your contact information, we encourage you to check this Privacy Policy section in the App Settings periodically for any updates.

---

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official GitHub repository or the support channels provided in the app information.
